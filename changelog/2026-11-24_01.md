## Why Care?

Email is where commitments, documents, and relationships pile up, and where
attention gets spent on dopamine hits instead of the work. kay-os is building
agents to work a person's whole archive. This study asks what already exists
before building more of it.

## What's New?

- **The question**, in the README: lighthouse projects for multi-account
  visibility, archive and backup, enhanced queries, triage to action,
  knowledge graphs and second brains, without giving up privacy, security, or
  personal and organizational ways of working.
- **msgvault**, pinned as the first reference: an offline, SQLite-backed
  email archive with deduplicated attachments, analytics, an API, and agent
  skills.
